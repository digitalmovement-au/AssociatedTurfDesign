## Matching Varieties to Each Part of Your Site
Most commercial sites have more than one set of conditions, and they can change dramatically from one side of a building to the other. A sunny playing field, a courtyard shaded by buildings and a high-profile entry lawn all ask for different things. Specifying one variety across the whole site often means some areas thrive while others thin out within a season, leaving the client with ongoing maintenance problems and the builder with call-backs.
Greenlees Park Couch and Kikuyu suit sunny, high-traffic areas such as sports fields, school grounds and open spaces. Greenlees Park also tolerates most herbicides, which makes weed control easier. Our team can review your plans and recommend a variety for each zone, taking into account sunlight, traffic, irrigation and how the area will be maintained once the project is handed over.

## Questions About Supplying Commercial Projects
Can you help us decide which variety to specify?
Yes. Send us your site plans or a description of each area, including sunlight, expected traffic and how the space will be maintained. We will recommend a variety for each zone, and where conditions vary, that may mean two or three varieties across different areas. Matching each variety to its conditions gives every area the best chance to thrive. Buffalo varieties also need less mowing, which can reduce ongoing maintenance for facility managers.
How do we calculate quantities for a large project?
Work out the square metres for each zone and each stage, leaving out paths, garden beds and hard surfaces. Measure each zone separately, as different areas may be specified with different varieties. If you send us your plans, our team can check your quantities before you place the order, which helps avoid shortfalls on laying day.
Can turf be supplied in stages over several months?
Yes. Staged supply is common on estates, schools and public spaces. We harvest each stage to order, so the turf is fresh every time, and you avoid pallets sitting on site while other areas are completed. Let us know the approximate dates for each stage when you request a quote, and we will plan harvests accordingly.
How do we handle program changes?
Call us as early as possible. Because we harvest to order, early notice lets us move a harvest and delivery to a new date. Once turf has been cut it needs to be laid quickly, so good communication protects both the turf and your budget. We will always try to find a solution that works for your program.
Do you provide establishment advice for site and facility teams?
Yes. We provide practical guidance on watering, first mowing and early use. New turf needs a deep soak straight after laying and daily watering for the first two to three weeks, so planning water and irrigation on site is essential. We are happy to share this guidance with whoever will maintain the site after handover. Established lawns generally do best with deep, less frequent watering and fertilising three to four times a year.
Is supply and laying available for commercial projects?
Yes. We can deliver on pallets for your own crew or supply and lay the turf for you, including machine-laid Maxi rolls for large open spaces. Talk to our team about the best mix of supply options for your commercial turf supply needs, and we will include each option clearly in your quote.

## Which Grass Should You Order?
The right grass depends mostly on sunlight. Soft-leaf buffalo varieties such as Matilda and Sir Walter perform with around three to four hours of direct sun a day, which makes them ideal for yards shaded by houses, fences and trees. Kikuyu and the couch grasses need full sun, ideally six hours or more, to stay thick.
Next, think about how the lawn will be used and how much mowing you want to do. Kikuyu recovers quickly from heavy wear but grows fast and needs regular mowing and edging. Buffalo grows more slowly, needs less mowing and its dense growth helps crowd out weeds. Couch gives the finest, most manicured finish in full sun but needs regular mowing to keep its neat look. If you are unsure, our team will happily recommend a variety for your yard.

## Questions About Ordering Grass Rolls
Is instant turf better than growing grass from seed?
For most homes, yes. Seed can take weeks to establish and uses more time and water while it grows, while instant turf gives you a dense, mature lawn the day it is laid. Turf also helps control dust, mud and erosion straight away, which is especially useful around new homes. It provides good weed protection from the start, although weeds still need to be managed as part of normal lawn care. Regular mowing and fertilising three to four times a year helps keep the lawn thick enough to crowd them out.
Which grass is best for a shady yard?
Soft-leaf buffalo is the best choice for shade. Matilda and Sir Walter perform with around three to four hours of direct sun a day, and Shademaster is up to 85% shade tolerant. Kikuyu and couch need full sun and tend to thin out in shady areas. If part of your yard is very heavily shaded, ask us to help assess it before you order.
Which grass is best for kids and dogs?
Matilda and Sir Walter combine a soft leaf with good wear tolerance, which makes them popular with families. In full sun, Kikuyu is very hard-wearing and repairs itself quickly. Kikuyu does spread quickly, so keep its edges trimmed near garden beds. It can also turn brown in winter in frosty areas. Our team can recommend the right option based on your sun, shade and how the lawn is used.
How much grass do I need to order?
Measure the length and width of each lawn area in metres and multiply them to get square metres, then add the areas together. Leave out garden beds and paths. If your lawn has curves or odd shapes, send us a sketch with measurements and we will help you work it out so you are not caught short halfway through laying.
How soon can I walk on my new grass?
Keep traffic to a minimum for the first few weeks while the roots take hold, and avoid walking on freshly watered areas. Most new lawns are ready for light use once they are firmly rooted and have had their first mow, usually three to four weeks after laying. Mow with sharp blades and only take the tips off for the first few cuts.
Do you deliver grass on weekends?
We deliver on Saturdays as well as Monday to Friday, which suits people planning to lay their lawn over the weekend. Booking ahead is a good idea, especially in the lead-up to Christmas. That is one of the busiest times of the year for turf. Call 1800 044 006 to arrange your grass delivery for the day that suits you.

## Questions About Choosing a Lawn Grass
What is the difference between Matilda and Sir Walter?
Both are premium soft-leaf buffalo grasses with excellent shade tolerance and suit Sydney conditions. Matilda has a finer leaf and a softer, more refined look, while Sir Walter has a broader, lush leaf. Both make excellent family lawns, and our team can help you choose based on your yard and the look you prefer. Matilda is our top recommendation for most Sydney homes.
Which grass needs the least mowing?
Soft-leaf buffalo varieties such as Matilda, Sir Walter and Shademaster grow more slowly than Kikuyu and couch, so they need less frequent mowing. Wintergreen Couch is also a slow-growing couch, but it needs regular mowing to keep its neat, fine appearance. Kikuyu grows the fastest and needs the most mowing in the warmer months. Well watered, fed and mown regularly, it is great for large areas.
Which grass stays greenest in winter?
Matilda and Sir Walter hold their colour well through Sydney's mild winters. Couch grasses can go dormant early in winter and may discolour in colder or frost-prone areas, and older Kikuyu lawns can brown off in frost. Your location and how much winter sun the lawn gets both play a part. Fertilising in autumn helps any lawn stay healthier through the cooler months.
Is Kikuyu a good choice for a small yard?
Kikuyu is excellent for large, sunny areas that get heavy wear, but it grows vigorously and can invade garden beds. For smaller yards with lots of garden edges, a buffalo or couch variety is usually easier to manage. If you do choose Kikuyu, keep the edges trimmed regularly to stop it spreading into beds.
Which grass handles the most wear?
In full sun, Kikuyu has excellent wear tolerance and recovers quickly, which is why it is used on playing fields, schools and backyards with dogs. Greenlees Park Couch also performs well under heavy wear conditions. Where there is partial shade, Matilda offers very good wear tolerance and self-repairing growth. Shademaster suits heavily shaded areas but has only fair wear tolerance.
Do you supply grass to landscapers and builders?
Yes. We supply landscapers, builders, schools and councils, deliver large orders on pallets and can schedule harvests around your program. Talk to us about regular orders and project pricing, and we will help you choose the right grass for each part of your site.

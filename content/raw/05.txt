Newly installed Sir Walter requires regular watering while its roots establish into the soil below.
Keep newly laid turf and the underlying soil adequately moist and avoid allowing it to dry out during the establishment period, particularly during warm or windy weather.
Once established, gradually move towards deeper, less frequent watering. Adjust watering according to rainfall, temperature, soil conditions and the appearance of the lawn.
### Mowing Sir Walter Turf
Sir Walter can generally be maintained at approximately 20–50mm.
Lawns growing in shaded areas can benefit from being maintained slightly longer, providing additional leaf area to capture the available sunlight.
During periods of active growth, mow regularly and avoid removing more than approximately one-third of the leaf blade in a single mow.

### Lawn Care Sydney
A healthy lawn is the centrepiece of any garden, and in Sydney’s climate, proper lawn care makes all the difference. From the hot summers to cooler winters, your grass needs the right maintenance to stay green, soft, and resilient year-round.
#### Essential Lawn Care Tips
**1. Watering**
Deep, infrequent watering encourages strong root systems. Early morning is best, and always check Sydney Water’s restrictions before you turn on the hose.
**2. Mowing**
- Keep blades sharp for a clean cut.
- Don’t mow too short, most lawns thrive at 30–40 mm.
- In summer, mow a little higher to protect grass from heat stress.
**3. Fertilising**
Sydney soils often need a boost. Use a slow-release fertiliser in early spring and autumn to keep your lawn nourished.
**4. Aeration**
Compacted soil stops roots from breathing. Aerate once or twice a year, especially for high-traffic lawns.
**5. Weed & Pest Control**
Bindii, clover, and lawn grub are common in Sydney lawns. Spot treat early before they take over.
#### Caring for Popular Sydney Lawn Varieties
- **Sir Walter Buffalo & Matilda Soft Leaf Buffalo**: Low-maintenance, shade tolerant, respond well to seasonal fertilising.
- **Kikuyu**: Fast-growing and tough, but needs regular mowing and edging.
- **Couch grasses (Greenlees Park & Wintergreen)**: Great for full sun but need consistent watering and dethatching.
#### Seasonal Lawn Care in Sydney
- **Spring**: Fertilise and mow regularly to kickstart growth.
- **Summer**: Water deeply, mow high, and watch for pests.
- **Autumn**: Fertilise to strengthen roots before winter.
- **Winter**: Mow less often, water sparingly.
#### Water-Wise Lawn Care
Sydney often faces water restrictions, but that doesn’t mean your lawn can’t thrive. Use rainwater tanks, wetting agents, and smart irrigation to keep your lawn healthy while conserving water.

### Wintergreen Couch Grass
Wintergreen is a fantastic couch grass used in many of the leading sporting stadiums around the world. Full sun required but has a beautiful soft feel underfoot. May discolour from dog urine but will recover and is a very hardy turf in full sun.

- Wintergreen was used on Parramatta Stadium and many other sporting arenas.
Wintergreen couch is a grass similar to kikuyu in colour. This will grow almost anywhere except in the mountains, shaded or very cold areas.
It is easier to manage as it is not as aggressive as other varieties and has good winter colour retention in warmer climates. Wintergreen performs well in large open areas. Wintergreen is suitable for plugging and can be introduced into finer lawns for conversion to a harder wearing lawn. Wintergreen has less thatching due to its open growth habits and carries a deeper colour.
Wintergreen is a very good grass for domestic lawns as well as sports fields and golf courses.
### Questions and Answers
#### Why is Wintergreen Couch Grass a good choice for Sydney’s climate?
Wintergreen Couch Grass is well-suited to Sydney’s climate because it thrives in warm weather while also tolerating cooler conditions in winter. Its deep root system helps it withstand dry spells, making it a great option for Sydney’s variable weather.
#### How does Wintergreen Couch handle drought conditions?
This variety of couch grass is known for its excellent drought resistance. Once established, it requires less water than many other turf types, making it an economical and environmentally friendly choice, especially during Sydney’s hot summers.
#### Is Wintergreen Couch Grass suitable for high-traffic areas?
Yes! Wintergreen Couch has a fine leaf blade and a dense growth habit, which makes it highly durable. It’s an excellent choice for backyards, sports fields, and parks where frequent foot traffic is expected.
#### How does Wintergreen Couch compare to other turf varieties in maintenance?
Wintergreen Couch is a low-maintenance grass but does require regular mowing to maintain its dense, fine-leaf appearance. It responds well to fertilisation and occasional dethatching, but its overall upkeep is relatively simple compared to some other turf varieties.
#### Is Wintergreen Couch resistant to weeds and pests?
Yes, its dense growth helps suppress weeds, reducing the need for herbicides. It also has good resistance to common pests, making it a practical choice for homeowners and landscapers looking for a hassle-free lawn.
#### How does Wintergreen Couch perform in shaded areas?
While it prefers full sun, Wintergreen Couch has better shade tolerance than many other couch varieties. It can handle light to moderate shade, but for heavily shaded areas, a more shade-tolerant variety like Buffalo grass may be a better option.
#### How quickly does Wintergreen Couch establish after laying?
It establishes relatively quickly, particularly in the warmer months. With proper watering and care, it develops a strong root system, providing a lush, green lawn in a short time.
#### Does Wintergreen Couch stay green during winter in Sydney?
While most warm-season grasses experience some dormancy in winter, Wintergreen Couch retains more colour than many other couch varieties, making it a great year-round option.
#### Is Wintergreen Couch suitable for Sydney’s coastal areas?
Yes! It has good salt tolerance, making it an excellent choice for properties near the coast or areas with slightly saline soil conditions.
#### What are the main advantages of choosing Wintergreen Couch Grass for a Sydney lawn?
The key benefits include: **Drought resistance**, needing less water once established; **High durability**, great for active families and pets; **Lush green colour** year-round with proper care; **Low maintenance** with minimal fertilisation and weed control required; **Good adaptability** to Sydney’s climate, from hot summers to mild winters.

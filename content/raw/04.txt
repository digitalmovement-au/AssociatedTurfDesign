## What Is Matilda Turf?
Matilda Turf is a premium **soft leaf buffalo grass developed in Australia for Australian conditions**.
Unlike older buffalo grasses with their broad, coarse leaves, Matilda has a softer and finer leaf structure that creates a comfortable lawn while retaining the toughness for which buffalo grasses are known.
Matilda performs well across a variety of growing conditions. It can be grown in full sun but is particularly useful for lawns receiving partial shade from homes, fences, trees and surrounding buildings.
Once established, Matilda also offers good drought tolerance and develops a dense growth habit that helps the lawn withstand normal residential wear.
For Sydney homeowners looking for a lawn that combines appearance, comfort and practicality, Matilda is an excellent all-round soft leaf buffalo variety.

## More About Matilda Turf
#### Matilda Turf for Children and Pets
Matilda is a popular choice for family lawns because it combines a **soft surface with good durability**.
Its fine, soft leaf is comfortable on bare feet and paws, while its dense growth provides an attractive surface for normal backyard activities.
For homes with dogs, the suitability of any turf will also depend on the size and number of dogs, traffic patterns and lawn area. Concentrated traffic in the same location can damage any living turf.
For a typical family backyard, however, Matilda provides an excellent balance between softness, appearance and durability.
#### Caring for Matilda Soft Leaf Buffalo
Matilda is relatively easy to maintain once established, but good lawn care will significantly improve its appearance and long-term performance.
### Watering Matilda Turf
Newly installed Matilda Turf needs regular watering while the roots establish into the soil below.
The turf and underlying soil should remain adequately moist during establishment. Avoid allowing freshly laid turf to dry out, particularly during warm or windy weather.
Once the lawn is established, move towards **deeper, less frequent watering**. This encourages the roots to grow deeper into the soil and helps develop a more drought-tolerant lawn.
Always adjust watering according to the season, rainfall, soil type and condition of the lawn.
### Mowing Matilda Turf
A mowing height of approximately **30–50mm** is suitable for Matilda.
Lawns growing in shaded areas should generally be kept slightly longer than lawns receiving full sun. The additional leaf area helps the grass make better use of the available sunlight.
During the warmer growing season, mowing may typically be required every 10–14 days, although the actual frequency will depend on growth rate and your preferred lawn height.
Avoid removing too much of the leaf in a single mow.
### Fertilising Matilda
Regular feeding helps Matilda maintain healthy growth and good colour.
Use a quality lawn fertiliser appropriate for buffalo grass and apply it according to the manufacturer’s instructions.
The amount and frequency required will depend on the season, soil and condition of the lawn. Avoid excessive fertiliser, particularly when the lawn is under heat or drought stress.
#### Preparing the Soil for Matilda Turf
Good preparation is one of the most important factors in establishing any new lawn.
Before laying Matilda Turf:
- Remove existing grass, weeds, rocks and debris.
- Prepare and level the soil.
- Correct drainage problems before laying turf.
- Add suitable soil or soil amendments where required.
- Apply an appropriate starter fertiliser or soil improver.
- Lay fresh turf as soon as possible after delivery.
- Water thoroughly immediately after installation.
Fresh turf should ideally be laid on the day it is delivered, particularly during warmer weather.
Correct preparation gives your new Matilda lawn the best opportunity to establish a strong root system and develop into a healthy, dense lawn.

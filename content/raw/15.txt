H: Laying Turf in Autumn in Sydney: Best Practices for Sir Walter Buffalo, Matilda Buffalo & Kikuyu
![Landscape Turf Supplies](U/2025/01/landscape-turf-supplies.jpg)
Autumn is one of the best times to lay new turf in Sydney. With milder temperatures and more consistent rainfall, the conditions are ideal for helping new grass establish strong roots before the cooler months of winter set in. Whether you’re considering Sir Walter Buffalo, Matilda Buffalo, or Kikuyu, here’s everything you need to know to ensure a lush, thriving lawn.
### Why Lay Turf in Autumn?
Laying turf in autumn offers several advantages:
- **Mild Temperatures:** The cooler conditions reduce stress on the turf compared to the extreme heat of summer.
- **More Rainfall:** Autumn showers provide natural hydration, reducing the need for frequent watering.
- **Strong Root Development:** The grass has time to establish a deep root system before winter dormancy, ensuring a healthy lawn in spring.
### Choosing the Right Turf Variety
Sydney’s climate is well-suited to a range of turf varieties, but Sir Walter Buffalo, Matilda Buffalo, and Kikuyu are particularly popular for their resilience and performance. Here’s how each one fares when laid in autumn:
#### Sir Walter Buffalo
- **Shade Tolerance:** Excellent – great for backyards with trees or shaded areas.
- **Drought Resistance:** High – retains moisture well during dry periods.
- **Softness:** Soft underfoot – ideal for families and pets.
- **Establishment Time:** Moderate – benefits from regular watering after installation.
#### Matilda Buffalo
- **Shade Tolerance:** Very good – performs well in partial shade.
- **Drought Resistance:** High – handles dry conditions well.
- **Softness:** Finer leaf than Sir Walter – luxurious feel underfoot.
- **Establishment Time:** Moderate – establishes well in autumn conditions with adequate care.
#### Kikuyu
- **Sun Requirement:** High – thrives in full sun.
- **Growth Rate:** Fast – quick establishment and recovery from wear.
- **Durability:** Very high – excellent for high-traffic areas.
- **Water Needs:** Moderate – requires regular watering initially but is drought-resistant once established.
### Steps for Laying Turf in Autumn
#### 1. Prepare the Soil
Proper preparation is crucial for successful turf installation. Follow these steps:
- Remove any weeds, rocks, or debris.
- Loosen the topsoil to a depth of 10–15cm.
- Add a quality turf underlay or sandy loam soil to improve drainage and nutrient retention.
- Level the area and lightly compact the soil to create a firm base.
#### 2. Lay the Turf
- Start laying the turf along a straight edge, staggering the joins like brickwork.
- Press the edges together firmly to prevent gaps.
- Use a roller or lightly walk over the turf to ensure good soil contact.
#### 3. Watering and Maintenance
- Water thoroughly immediately after laying the turf.
- For the first two weeks, water daily unless there is significant rainfall.
- Reduce watering frequency once the turf begins to establish.
- Avoid mowing until the grass is well-rooted, usually after 3–4 weeks.
### Caring for Your New Lawn Through Winter
- **Minimal Mowing:** Growth slows in winter, so mow only when necessary.
- **Fertilising:** Apply a slow-release fertiliser before winter to support healthy growth.
- **Weed Control:** Keep an eye out for weeds and remove them promptly to prevent competition for nutrients.
### Final Thoughts
Laying turf in autumn in Sydney is an excellent choice for homeowners wanting a healthy, green lawn year-round. Sir Walter Buffalo, Matilda Buffalo, and Kikuyu each have unique benefits, so choosing the right variety depends on your lawn’s conditions and needs. With proper preparation and care, your new turf will establish strong roots before winter and be ready to thrive in spring.

D: Laying new turf is an exciting step towards creating a lush, green lawn in your Sydney home or business. However, before you get to the fun part, proper site preparation is key to ensuring your turf thrives.
OG: U/2019/05/associated-turf-logo.png

H: Site Preparation Before Laying New Turf in Sydney
Laying new turf is an exciting step towards creating a lush, green lawn in your Sydney home or business. However, before you get to the fun part, proper site preparation is key to ensuring your turf thrives. Skipping this crucial step can result in uneven growth, weed invasion, and a less-than-ideal lawn. In this blog, we'll guide you through the essential steps for preparing your site before laying new turf in Sydney.
### 1. Assess Your Soil Quality
Before doing anything, take the time to assess your soil. Sydney’s climate can vary significantly, and this means different soil types may require specific treatment. The ideal soil for turf should be well-draining, slightly acidic to neutral in pH, and rich in organic matter.
**Tips:**
- Test your soil pH. If it's too acidic or too alkaline, you may need to amend it with lime (to raise pH) or sulfur (to lower pH).
- If your soil is heavy clay, consider aerating it and adding organic compost to improve drainage.
- Sandy soils may need to be enriched with compost or organic matter to improve water retention and fertility.
### 2. Clear the Area
Clearing your lawn area is crucial for ensuring that your turf has room to root and grow without competition from existing plants or debris. This includes removing any old turf, weeds, rocks, sticks, or trash. If you're re-turfing, it's important to dig up the old grass and its roots to avoid them regrowing and interfering with your new turf.
**Tips:**
- Use a shovel or turf cutter to remove old grass and weeds.
- Consider using an organic herbicide to tackle any persistent weeds before you begin laying turf.
### 3. Level the Ground
A level surface is important for the aesthetic and health of your new lawn. Uneven ground can lead to areas where water pools or the turf doesn't establish properly. Use a rake to smooth out any high or low spots and make sure the surface is even.
**Tips:**
- If there are large uneven spots, you may need to add soil to fill in low areas.
- If the area is sloped, you might want to consider building a retaining wall or using soil to level out the gradient.
### 4. Improve Drainage (If Necessary)
Sydney’s weather can be unpredictable, so proper drainage is essential to prevent your turf from becoming waterlogged. If your soil has poor drainage, it’s essential to address this issue before laying your turf. Poor drainage can lead to soggy patches that will harm the turf and promote the growth of mould and mildew.
**Tips:**
- If you notice standing water after heavy rainfall, consider installing drainage pipes or creating small swales to direct water away from the turf.
- Aerating the soil can also help improve drainage and provide a better environment for the turf's roots.
### 5. Fertilise the Soil
Once your area is cleared and levelled, it's time to fertilise the soil. Adding a quality fertiliser will give your new turf a solid start. Look for a slow-release fertiliser designed for lawns, which will provide nutrients over a longer period.
**Tips:**
- Use a fertiliser rich in nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium to encourage healthy root development.
- Avoid over-fertilising, as this can burn the roots or encourage excessive top growth without strong root establishment.
### 6. Water the Area
Before you lay your turf, it's important to water the soil lightly. This helps to settle the soil and gives the turf a moist bed to establish its roots. Avoid making the soil too soggy, as this can create a muddy mess and make the turf harder to lay.
**Tips:**
- Water the soil a day or two before laying the turf, so it's moist but not wet.
- If you're laying turf in hot weather, consider laying the turf early in the morning or late in the afternoon to prevent it from drying out too quickly.
### 7. Lay Your Turf Properly
Now that your site is properly prepared, it's time to lay your turf! Lay the rolls of turf in a brickwork pattern, ensuring the edges are tightly butted together. Avoid overlapping the turf, as this can restrict growth. Make sure the turf is level with the surrounding soil.
**Tips:**
- Start laying turf along one edge of the area, and work your way across, ensuring the pieces fit snugly together.
- Water the turf immediately after laying it to help it settle and reduce any stress from transplanting.
### 8. Maintain Your New Lawn
After laying your turf, the work doesn’t stop there. Consistent care is essential to help the turf establish strong roots and grow into a beautiful, healthy lawn. Regular watering, mowing, and fertilising are important steps in maintaining your new lawn.
**Tips:**
- Water your turf deeply but infrequently, especially during the first few weeks, to encourage deep root growth.
- Avoid mowing too soon; wait until the grass has rooted well, usually around two to three weeks after installation.
- Fertilise your lawn regularly to ensure it stays healthy and green.
### Conclusion
Preparing the site before laying new turf in Sydney is crucial for achieving a beautiful, healthy lawn that will thrive in the city’s climate. By assessing your soil, clearing the area, levelling the ground, improving drainage, and fertilising the soil, you’re setting up your turf for success. With the right preparation and ongoing care, you’ll have a lush, green lawn that adds beauty and value to your property.

## Preparing Your Garden for New Turf
When it comes to successful turf installation, preparation is key. Before your turf arrives:
- **Clear the area** of weeds, old grass, stones and debris.
- **Level and loosen the soil** to ensure healthy drainage.
- **Enrich the soil** with quality topsoil or turf underlay for stronger growth.
- **Rake to a smooth surface**, eliminating lumps and bumps for an even lawn finish.
Taking these steps ensures your new lawn has the best chance to thrive from day one.
## Laying Your Turf – Lawn Installation Tips
Once your soil is ready, it’s time to lay your turf. Here are some essential **lawn installation tips**:
- **Lay turf rolls immediately** after delivery to prevent drying out.
- **Stagger the joints** in a brickwork pattern for a seamless finish.
- **Press down firmly** or use a roller to eliminate air pockets.
- **Water thoroughly** as soon as the turf is laid, ensuring moisture reaches deep into the soil.
Following these simple practices will help your garden turf establish quickly and evenly.
## Watering and Ongoing Care
In spring and early summer, newly laid turf needs consistent watering to establish strong roots. Water daily for the first couple of weeks, then gradually reduce as the turf settles. Avoid mowing until the grass is around 5–7cm tall, and make sure your mower blades are sharp to avoid damaging fresh growth.

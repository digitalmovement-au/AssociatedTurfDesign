## Expert Answers to Common Turf Questions
Not sure which turf suits your property? Here are the questions we hear most. Our team is always happy to help.
Which turf type is best for shade?
Buffalo grasses like Matilda, Sir Walter and Shademaster are the best performers in shady areas. Matilda and Sir Walter thrive with as little as 3 to 4 hours of direct sunlight. Kikuyu and Couch varieties such as Wintergreen and Greenlees Park need full sun (6+ hours daily).
Which turf has the best drought tolerance?
Sir Walter and Matilda Soft Leaf Buffalo are both excellent for drought tolerance due to their deep root systems and broad leaf blades that retain moisture. Kikuyu is also drought tough, though it may brown off more quickly in extreme heat.
What is the best turf for high wear areas or pets?
Kikuyu is very hardy and fast repairing, making it a top choice for active families, pets and high traffic lawns. Sir Walter and Matilda are also durable but recover more slowly from heavy wear.
Which turf is the lowest maintenance?
Sir Walter and Matilda Soft Leaf Buffalo are considered low maintenance due to their low thatch build up, slow vertical growth and minimal fertiliser needs. Kikuyu and Couch varieties grow quickly and require more frequent mowing and edging.
What is the difference between Matilda and Sir Walter?
Both are premium soft leaf Buffalo varieties with similar traits, but Matilda is a semi dwarf variety with finer leaves and a denser growth habit. It can feel softer underfoot and may need slightly less mowing.
Which turf establishes the fastest after laying?
Kikuyu establishes the fastest thanks to its vigorous growth habit. Couch grasses also establish quickly in warm weather. Buffalo varieties establish more slowly but form a dense, weed resistant lawn once mature.

## Why Turf Should Not Wait on Site
Turf is a living product, and from the moment it is cut the clock is ticking on its condition. The time between harvest and laying makes a real difference to how quickly turf establishes, and prompt installation helps minimise stress on the grass. Fresh turf roots faster and stays greener. The best results come when turf is laid on the day it is delivered and watered in straight away, which is why delivery timing matters as much as turf quality.
That is why every commercial turf delivery from Associated Turf is matched to your laying day, and we encourage you to tell us early if the program changes. If your site is not ready because of weather or other trades, call us as soon as possible and we will work with you to adjust the cut and delivery. It also pays to have water connected and ready, because new turf needs a deep soak as soon as it goes down and daily watering for the first two to three weeks. Early morning watering, between 5am and 9am, gives the best results.

## A Buffalo for Heavily Shaded Yards
Many Sydney properties have shaded sections caused by fences, trees or surrounding buildings. Shademaster is worth considering for heavily shaded courtyards. It is up to 85% shade tolerant and has good resistance to fungal disease.
Where shade is lighter, Matilda and Sir Walter perform with around three to four hours of direct sun a day. Heavily shaded yards may suit Shademaster better, while sunny, high-traffic areas can suit Kikuyu or couch. Very heavy shade should be assessed before you choose any turf, so ask our team to help.
## Caring for Shademaster
Shademaster requires minimal mowing, and like the other soft leaf buffalo varieties it grows more slowly than Kikuyu and couch, so it needs less mowing and fertilising. Its dense growth helps crowd out weeds.
Shademaster suits heavily shaded areas but has only fair wear tolerance, so it is best kept away from the busiest play and traffic areas. Where a shaded lawn also takes heavy wear, Matilda offers very good wear tolerance and self-repairing growth.
### Questions and Answers
#### How much shade can Shademaster handle?
Shademaster is up to 85% shade tolerant, which makes it the most shade tolerant variety we grow. It also thrives in full sun.
#### Is Shademaster good for children?
Yes. Its thick, spongy turf makes it a great all rounder that is particularly kind to children.
#### How does Shademaster compare with Matilda and Sir Walter?
All three are soft leaf buffalo varieties. Matilda and Sir Walter perform with around three to four hours of direct sun a day and have very good wear tolerance. Shademaster handles heavier shade but has only fair wear tolerance.

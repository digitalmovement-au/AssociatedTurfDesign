Do I need to prepare the soil before my turf arrives?
Yes. Remove existing grass, weeds and debris, improve the soil with organic matter or quality topsoil, and rake the area level with a gentle slope away from buildings. A starter fertiliser or soil improver applied before laying and watered in lightly helps settle the soil and activate nutrients. Once the turf is down, give it a deep soak straight away.

Yes. Our farm is open Saturday from 7am to 12pm as well as Monday to Friday from 8am to 4.30pm. ### Lawn Watering in Sydney
Keeping your lawn looking green and healthy in Sydney’s unique climate requires the right watering practices. With hot summers, occasional droughts, and water restrictions, knowing when and how to water your lawn can make all the difference.
#### How Often Should You Water?
- **New lawns**: Water immediately after laying, then daily for a minimum ½ hour per day or until soil underneath is fully wet for 2 weeks, then water as need to keep moisture.
- **Established lawns**: Water deeply 2–3 times per week in summer, less in cooler months.
- **Morning watering is best**: Between 5 am and 9 am reduces evaporation and gives grass time to dry before nightfall.
#### Sydney Conditions to Keep in Mind
- **Summer**: Hot, dry days mean more frequent watering. Aim for deep soakings rather than short, shallow sprays.
- **Winter**: Most lawns only need occasional watering, as natural rainfall is usually enough.
- **Restrictions**: Sydney Water often enforces water restrictions. Always check the latest guidelines before turning on the sprinklers.
#### Tips for Efficient Lawn Watering
- **Soak, don’t sprinkle** – Encourage deep root growth by watering thoroughly once or twice a week instead of daily light watering.
- **Use a timer or smart controller** – Helps avoid overwatering and ensures even coverage.
- **Check your soil** – Sandy soils dry out faster than clay, so adjust watering frequency accordingly.
- **Mulch and aerate** – Keeps soil moist for longer and improves water penetration.
- **Targeted watering** – Use drip irrigation or soaker hoses for maximum efficiency.
#### Watering Different Lawn Varieties
- **Sir Walter Buffalo & Matilda Soft Leaf Buffalo**: Thrive with deep, infrequent watering.
- **Kikuyu**: Hardy, but benefits from consistent moisture in summer.
- **Couch grasses (Greenlees Park, Wintergreen)**: Need regular watering during hot months to avoid browning.
#### Conserving Water in Sydney
With droughts and restrictions a regular part of Sydney life, smart lawn care is essential:
- Collect rainwater in tanks.
- Use wetting agents to improve soil water retention.
- Mow at a higher setting to reduce evaporation.

Does Wintergreen keep its colour in winter?
Most warm-season grasses experience some dormancy in winter, but Wintergreen retains more colour than many other couch varieties, particularly in warmer climates. It is suited to Sydney's climate because it thrives in warm weather while also tolerating cooler conditions in winter.
Is Wintergreen suitable near the coast?
Yes. Wintergreen has good salt tolerance, which makes it an excellent choice for properties near the coast or areas with slightly saline soil conditions. As with any couch, it needs full sun to perform at its best, so check how much direct sunlight the area receives. Our team can help you decide whether it suits your property.
How drought tolerant is Wintergreen?
Wintergreen is known for its drought resistance. Its deep root system helps it withstand dry spells, and once established it requires less water than many other turf types. How much maintenance does Wintergreen need?

Can Wintergreen be added to an existing lawn?
